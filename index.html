🏷️ Local Rate — Tola / 11.664 wala

1 Tola = 11.664 gram hota hai (Pakistan ka local standard). Actual item ki calculation isi se hoti hai — upar wala Ounce/31.1 nahi. 24K per gram = 24K tola rate ÷ 11.664 Kisi karat ka gram = 24K per gram × (karat ÷ 24) Misaal: 24K tola = 4,32,500 4,32,500 ÷ 11.664 = 37,079 (24K gram) 37,079 × (21 ÷ 24) = 32,445 (21K gram)

❓ Shop wala zyada kyun bolta hai?

  • Kuch shop tola ko 11.664 ki jagah 11.3 gram maan lete hain → per gram rate upar aa jaata hai (customer se zyada paisa).
  • Ya wo base rate hi thoda upar rakhte hain, ya wastage/margin ko per-gram rate mein chhupa dete hain.
  • Match karna ho to neeche "Per Gram Rate" field mein shop wala rate (jaise 33,500) daal do — sab usi hisab se chalega.

💎 Nag / Stone (Patthar) ka dhyaan

Ring/set mein jo stone (nag) laga ho wo sona nahi — uski gold value 0 hoti hai. Total weight mein se stone ka weight minus karo, warna stone ke bhi sone ke paise lag jayenge. Net Gold = Total weight − Stone weight Gold price sirf Net Gold pe lagta hai (Stone deduction field Sell Mode mein hai.)

🧮 Final Price ka formula

Gold value = Net gram × per-gram rate + Wastage = (% ya fixed) + Making = (fixed ya per gram × weight) + Tax/GST = (% subtotal ya sirf gold pe) ───────────────────────────── = Total Price Sell Mode mein wastage/kaat minus hoti hai, add nahi.
